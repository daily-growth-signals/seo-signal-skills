What SignalDig does

Three focused skills turn traceable SEO and social evidence into a clear, testable content decision.

01

Research SEO demand signals

research-seo-signals

Find evidence-backed SEO demand for keywords, domains, markets, and languages — Google Trends, keyword volume, and SERP data.

02

Retrieve social signals

research-social-signals

Validate platform inputs and pull traceable public conversations, creator histories, account content, and native metrics from X, Reddit, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, LinkedIn, and WeChat.

03

Decide content opportunities

decide-content-opportunities

Turn completed SEO and social evidence into a recommended content direction — with confidence, counter-evidence, alternatives, and a next test.

When to use it

  • Pick the right keyword — confirm which keyword or topic has real, traceable search demand before you write.
  • Validate a trending topic — check the evidence when a topic is buzzing on social platforms, before investing in content.
  • Plan your content roadmap — turn research evidence into a prioritized content direction with a clear next test.
  • Research a new market or language — gauge keyword, domain, and market demand across languages.

Simple Download Installation

Download the skills zip file, unzip it, and paste the skill folders into your AI agent. No coding or command line needed — just follow each step below.

Step 1: Download the skills

Download seo-signal-skills.zip ↓

Contains all three skill folders, ready to use

1

Click the button above — a GitHub "Releases" page opens in a new tab.

2

Find the newest version (marked Latest) and click the seo-signal-skills-*.zip file to download it.

3

The file is saved to your Downloads folder — a download prompt also appears in your browser.

Step 2: Unzip the downloaded file

1

Open your Downloads folder and find the seo-signal-skills-*.zip file.

2

Windows: right-click the file → choose "Extract All…" → click Extract. Mac: just double-click the file — it unzips automatically.

3

A new folder with a similar name appears (e.g. seo-signal-skills-1.2.0). Double-click it to open it.

4

Inside you will see exactly three skill folders: research-seo-signals, research-social-signals and decide-content-opportunities.

Step 3: Put the skills into your AI agent

Every AI client (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf…) has its own skills folder. Anything inside it becomes a skill your AI can use. Copy all three skill folders there.

Can't find the skills folder? Do this:
1

Open your home folder. Mac: press Cmd + Shift + H in Finder. Windows: open File Explorer and go to C:\Users\YourUserName.

2

The skills folder is hidden (its name starts with a dot). To see hidden files: Mac — press Cmd + Shift + .; Windows — click View at the top, then tick Hidden items.

3

Look for the folder named after your AI client: .claude, .codex, .cursor or .windsurf. Open it, then open the skills sub-folder inside.

4

No skills folder? Right-click → New Folder → name it exactly skills.

Claude Code

~/.claude/skills/

Codex

~/.codex/skills/

Cursor

~/.cursor/skills/

Windsurf

~/.windsurf/skills/

Open the folder you unzipped (the one holding the three skill folders)

Done! Your skills folder should now contain the three folders. Fully quit and reopen your AI app so it notices the new skills.

Step 4: Connect the data service (required)

The skills fetch real data through SignalDig's data service (called MCP). Without this step the AI cannot get real data — so this step cannot be skipped.

MCP is like a bridge between your AI and SignalDig's data service. Here is the easiest way to set it up:

1

Open any skill folder you unzipped (e.g. research-seo-signals) and open its SKILL.md file with a text editor (Notepad / TextEdit).

2

Copy the whole content of SKILL.md, paste it into your AI chat, and tell it: "Please configure this MCP connection for me."

3

The AI will guide you or configure it directly. If it asks for an API Key, click the button below to get a free one.

Get your API key from your SignalDig workspace dashboard. Get your API Key →

The API Key is like your data pass for SignalDig services. Keep it private — don't paste it into chats you don't trust.
